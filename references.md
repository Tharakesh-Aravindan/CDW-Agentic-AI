# Data Source References

> All datasets listed below were used in the practicum project *Circular Economy in Construction Waste Management and Policy Recommendation using Agentic AI* (Suresh Thanuja & Neupane). Citations are formatted for the dissertation references section.

---

## EPA Licensee Waste Data (2021–2025)

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *EPA Licensee Waste Data — Reporting Year 2021*. Dublin: EPA, 2022. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *EPA Licensee Waste Data — Reporting Year 2022*. Dublin: EPA, 2023. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *EPA Licensee Waste Data — Reporting Year 2023*. Dublin: EPA, 2024. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *EPA Licensee Waste Data — Reporting Year 2024*. Dublin: EPA, 2025. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *EPA Licensee Waste Data — Reporting Year 2025*. Dublin: EPA, 2026. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project files:** `EPA-Licensee-Waste-Data-2021.xlsx` through `EPA-Licensee-Waste-Data-2025.xlsx`

**Used for:** Facility-level operational data on waste accepted, treated, and transferred by licensed operators in Ireland, filtered to List of Waste (LoW) Chapter 17 (construction & demolition waste) and classified by EU Waste Framework Directive R/D treatment codes to compute per-licensee recovery rates against the EU 70 % target.

## CSO National Waste Statistics — Waste Generated (GWA01)

Central Statistics Office (Ireland), *Environmental Indicators — Waste Generated by Economic Sector (NACE Rev. 2), Hazardousness and Waste Category, 2004–2020* (Table GWA01). Cork: CSO. Available at: https://data.cso.ie/table/GWA01. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `waste.csv`

**Used for:** National-level waste generation totals by NACE sector (biennial, 2004–2020), from which the NACE F (Construction) series is extracted to provide long-run macro context for CDW trends predating the EPA facility-level data era.

---

## CSO National Waste Statistics — Waste Treated (GWA02)

Central Statistics Office (Ireland), *Environmental Indicators — Waste Treated by Hazardousness, Waste Category and Waste Management Operation, 2004–2020* (Table GWA02). Cork: CSO. Available at: https://data.cso.ie/table/GWA02. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `waste_treated.csv`

**Used for:** National treatment-route benchmarks (landfill, recycling, backfilling, energy recovery, incineration) by waste category, providing the disposal-vs-recovery baseline that the Forecaster and ScenarioEvaluator agents will use for trend modelling.

---

## CSO Production in Building and Construction Index (BEA04)

Central Statistics Office (Ireland), *Production in Building and Construction Index (Base Year 2015 = 100)* (Table BEA04). Cork: CSO. Available at: https://data.cso.ie/table/BEA04. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `cso_construction_indicators.csv` (columns `pbci_total_volume_index`, `pbci_residential_volume_index`, `pbci_civil_engineering_volume_index` and associated YoY growth rates)

**Used for:** Exogenous construction activity proxy (annual volume indices for total, residential, and civil engineering output, 2015–2024) serving as a demand-side driver in the Forecaster agent's CDW generation predictions.

---

## CSO New Dwelling Completions (NDA02)

Central Statistics Office (Ireland), *New Dwelling Completions by Type* (Table NDA02). Cork: CSO. Available at: https://data.cso.ie/table/NDA02. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `cso_construction_indicators.csv` (columns `new_dwelling_completions`, `new_dwelling_single`, `new_dwelling_scheme`, `new_dwelling_apartments`, `new_dwelling_completions_yoy_pct`)

**Used for:** Annual new dwelling completions by type (single house, scheme, apartment, 2015–2025) as a complementary activity indicator linking housing construction volume to CDW generation forecasts.

---

## EPA C&D Data Archive Summary

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *Construction & Demolition Waste Data Archive — Summary Statistics, 2004–2023*. Dublin: EPA. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/monitoring--assessment/waste/national-waste-statistics/construction--demolition/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `C&D-Data-Archive-Summary-Excel.xlsx`

**Used for:** Cross-validation of pipeline-computed CDW recovery rates against EPA-published national aggregates; provides official totals for C&D waste generated, collected (by material stream: metals, wood/glass/plastic, concrete/brick/tile/gypsum, bituminous mixtures, mixed, soil & stone), and treated (recycled, energy recovery, backfilling, landfill disposal).

## Irish Landfill Levy Legislation

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Regulations 2002* (S.I. No. 86 of 2002). Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2002/si/86/made/en/print. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2013*. Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2013/si/194/made/en/print. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2023*. Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2024*. Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Circular Economy (Waste Recovery Levy) Regulations 2023*. Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications (Ireland), *Circular Economy (Waste Recovery Levy) Regulations 2024*. Dublin: Stationery Office. Available at: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `policy_levy_timeline.csv`

**Used for:** Encoding the full timeline of Irish landfill levy rates (€15–€85/tonne, 2002–2024) and waste recovery levy introduction (€10/tonne, 2023–2024), including the historically significant transition from C&D exemption to C&D inclusion in September 2024, as policy levers for the ScenarioEvaluator agent.

## EPA End-of-Waste and By-Product Decisions

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *National End-of-Waste Criteria for Recycled Aggregates* (Decision EoW-N001/2023). Dublin: EPA, October 2023. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/publications/licensing--permitting/waste/-national-end-of-waste-criteria-recycled-aggregates.php. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *National By-Product Criteria for Site-Won Asphalt (Road Planings)* (Regulation 27 Decision). Dublin: EPA, November 2023. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/licensing/waste/by-products-regulation-27/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

Environmental Protection Agency (Ireland), *National By-Product Criteria for Greenfield Soil and Stone* (Regulation 27 Decision). Dublin: EPA, August 2024. Available at: https://www.epa.ie/news-releases/news-releases-2024/epa-national-criteria-to-help-reduce-construction-waste---.php. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `policy_eow_byproduct_decisions.csv`

**Used for:** Documenting Irish regulatory decisions that reclassify specific C&D materials (recycled aggregates, site-won asphalt, greenfield soil & stone) from waste to product/by-product status, which directly affects recovery-rate calculations and is contextual input for the PolicyRecommender agent.

## Eurostat — Waste Generation by Economic Activity (env_wasgen)

Eurostat, *Generation of waste by waste category, hazardousness and NACE Rev. 2 activity* (Table env_wasgen), filtered to NACE Construction sector. Luxembourg: Publications Office of the European Union. Available at: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/env_wasgen/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `eurostat_cd_waste_generation.csv`

**Used for:** EU-wide cross-country benchmarking of construction & demolition waste generation volumes (tonnes, biennial 2010–2022, all EU member states) to contextualise Ireland's CDW output relative to EU-27 averages and individual peer countries.

---

## Eurostat — Waste Treatment by Type of Operation (env_wastrt)

Eurostat, *Treatment of waste by waste category, hazardousness and waste management operations* (Table env_wastrt), filtered to mineral and construction waste categories. Luxembourg: Publications Office of the European Union. Available at: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/env_wastrt/. Accessed: 12 May 2026.

**Project file:** `eurostat_cd_waste_treatment.csv`

**Used for:** EU-wide cross-country benchmarking of C&D waste treatment profiles (disposal, recovery, recycling, backfilling, incineration breakdown by country, biennial 2010–2022) to compare Ireland's recovery performance against the EU 70 % CDW recovery target and peer-country achievements.
